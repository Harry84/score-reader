Privacy Policy

The Discord bot reads message content and image attachments only in the channels it is configured to monitor, in order to extract match results (players, teams, scores) and Discord IDs needed to manage team rosters and captains. This data is stored to compute the stats and ELO ladders shown on this site. It is not sold or shared with third parties. For questions or data removal requests, contact the maintainer via the GitHub repository.
